## CSLB — California Contractors State License Board

The official licensing authority for contractors in California. Electricians hold a **C-10 Electrical Contractor** classification.

**License Verification:** https://www.cslb.ca.gov/onlineservices/checklicenseII/checklicense.aspx
**Data Portal / Contractor List:** https://www.cslb.ca.gov/onlineservices/dataportal/ContractorList

There are approximately **17,666 active C-10 electrical contractors** in California (as of 2023 data).

### How to Verify a C-10 License on CSLB:
1. Visit https://www.cslb.ca.gov
2. Click "Check a License"
3. Search by contractor name, license number, business name, or city
4. Review license status, bond information, workers' comp, and any complaints
