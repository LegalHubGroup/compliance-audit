MCLE Compliance & Tracking

25 hours every 3 years including ethics, elimination of bias, and competence credits. False certification triggers full charges.

Competence & Diligence

Rules 1.1 and 1.3 — the most-cited violations in California discipline cases. Patterns of neglect compound rapidly.

Penalty: Suspension to Disbarment

Client Communication

Rule 1.4 requires prompt updates on significant developments. Communication failures are a top-3 complaint driver.

Penalty: Reproval to Suspension

Fee Agreement Compliance

Rule 1.5 — written agreements required for contingency. Advance fees and flat fees must now be held in trust until earned.

Penalty: Fee Disgorgement + Discipline

Conflict of Interest Checks

Penalty: Disqualification + Discipline

CCPA / Data Privacy
