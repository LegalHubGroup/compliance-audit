About Our Studio

Where tradition meets creative vision

Founded in Van Nuys, Hye Art Glass brings together generations of glass artistry with contemporary design. Our studio is dedicated to creating one-of-a-kind glass pieces that transform spaces with light, color, and beauty.

From churches and historic buildings to modern homes and commercial spaces, every project receives the same meticulous attention to detail and passion for the craft that has defined our work for over two decades.

Our Services

Complete art glass solutions

From initial concept to final installation, we handle every aspect of your art glass project with expert precision.

◆

Custom Stained Glass

Bespoke stained glass windows and panels designed and crafted to your exact specifications. We work with you from concept sketches to the finished, installed piece.

✦

Fused Glass Art

Kiln-formed glass art pieces including bowls, wall panels, sculptures, and decorative accents. Each piece is a unique expression of color and texture.

⚙

Glass Restoration

Expert restoration and repair of antique and historic stained glass windows. We preserve original artistry while reinforcing structural integrity.

■

Architectural Glass

Large-scale architectural glass installations for commercial buildings, hotels, restaurants, and public spaces. Designed for maximum visual impact.

◯

Glass Etching & Carving

Precision sandblasting and hand-carving techniques to create detailed designs on flat glass surfaces for doors, partitions, and decorative panels.

◎

Leaded Glass Panels

Traditional lead came and copper foil techniques for cabinet doors, transoms, sidelights, and decorative window inserts in any style.

How We Work

From vision to reality

Our proven process ensures your art glass project is completed on time, on budget, and beyond expectations.

1

Consultation

We meet with you to understand your vision, assess the space, and discuss design possibilities, materials, and budget.

2

Design

Our artists create detailed renderings and color studies for your approval. We refine every detail until the design is perfect.

3

Crafting

Using traditional techniques and premium materials, we hand-cut, assemble, and finish each piece in our Van Nuys studio.

4

Installation

Our team professionally installs your finished piece with meticulous care, ensuring perfect fit and lasting beauty.
